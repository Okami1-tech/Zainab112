A promise I want to keep

I want to be
a safe place for you.

A person you can talk to without pretending. Someone who listens when your heart is full, stays patient when a day is difficult, and celebrates the little things that make you smile.

Not perfect. Present. Sincere. Trying.
In the little things

Your smile is a joy.
Your feelings matter, too.

I want to make you laugh, yes—but I also want to understand you. You never have to hide a hard day just to protect my happiness. There is room for all of you with me.

When I don't know the perfect words

I'll keep learning
how to love you well.

To be gentler when you're hurting. More patient when things are difficult. Grateful when life is beautiful. Honest when a conversation is hard. And thoughtful enough to remember that love is shown in actions, again and again.

A du'a from me

Ya Allah, teach me
to love her well.

When she is hurting, teach me gentleness. When things are difficult, teach me patience. When things are good, teach me gratitude. Make my intentions sincere and my actions kind. Place goodness, peace, and barakah in what is between us, and guide us always toward what pleases You.

With love, Muhammad ♡
Alhamdulillah for the blessing of knowing you
